What's in it

  • Chrome WAPOW logo with comic-book WAPOW bursts and glitch effects
  • Synthwave sun, wireframe mountains and falling code rain
  • GPU-rendered Evo 8 time-attack car with neon edges and spinning wheels
  • Real-time airflow particles that react to the wing, diffuser, splitter and wheels
  • The car overtakes the camera every so often for a close look
  • Works across multiple monitors
  • Auto graphics quality, so it runs smoothly on older PCs too

How to install

  1. Download and unzip.
  2. Move WapowGrid.scr to a folder you'll keep, such as Documents.
  3. Right-click WapowGrid.scr and choose Install.
  4. Pick a wait time in Screen Saver Settings and click OK.

“Windows protected your PC”? Windows shows this for screensavers that aren't sold through a store. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (built into Windows 11; already on almost every Windows 10 PC)
  • Any graphics card from the last 10 years

Settings

In Screen Saver Settings, click Settings… to choose graphics quality (Auto, High, Medium, Low), the car's neon colour, and how often it overtakes.

Uninstall

Choose “(None)” in Screen Saver Settings, then delete WapowGrid.scr. For a full clean-up, also delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\WapowGrid.

FAQ

The screen goes black instead of showing the animation.
Install the free Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.
It stutters.
Open Settings… and choose Low. Auto mode also steps down on its own after a few seconds.
Does it go online?
Only to load three fonts from Google Fonts. It doesn't collect or send anything about you. It works fine offline; the WAPOW lettering just uses a plain bold font instead.
Mac or Linux?
Windows only for now.
